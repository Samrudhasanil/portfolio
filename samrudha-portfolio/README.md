# Samrudha Sanil — Creative Personal Portfolio Website

An interactive, modern, and artistic personal portfolio website created for **Samrudha Sanil**, a 4th-year B.Tech Computer Science and Design student at **Vimal Jyothi Engineering College** (Graduating in 2027).

---

## 🌟 Concept & Philosophy: *"Where Code Meets Creativity"*

Unlike a conventional student developer resume, this portfolio functions as an **interactive digital art gallery and design studio**. It is engineered to highlight Samrudha's primary identity as a **creative-focused designer** who leverages technology as an expressive canvas.

The core narrative demonstrates her depth in **UI/UX Design, Illustration, Poster Design, and Creative Web Experiences**, while framing her Computer Science background as a technical superpower that allows her to build seamless bridges with software engineers.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Sections

### 1. Hero / Home Section
- **Artistic Portrait Showcase**: Framed within an organic morphing blob frame with animated gradient glows and floating badges (`Vimal Jyothi CSD '27`, `Figma Artisan`, `Creative Tech`).
- **Interactive Role Rotator**: Animated typewriter cycling through *"UI/UX Designer • Digital Illustrator • Creative Poster Designer • Web Creator"*.
- **Quick Stat Metrics**: 4th Year CSD, 50+ Posters & Visuals, Figma Core Specialization.
- **Dynamic CTAs**: "Explore My Work" (smooth scrolls to `#work`) and "Let’s Connect" (scrolls to `#contact`).

### 2. Motion & Interaction Design
- **Interactive Magnetic Cursor**: Smooth cursor dot and trailing fluid ring that expands when hovering over interactive elements.
- **Generative Background Canvas**: Ambient constellation particle system floating dynamically in response to window activity.
- **3D Card Perspective Tilt**: Interactive project, poster, and service cards that subtly tilt in 3D based on mouse position.
- **Scroll-Triggered Reveals**: Smooth Intersection Observer reveals for cards, headings, and images.
- **Reduced Motion Support**: Fully respects `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` for accessibility.

### 3. Light & Dark Themes
- Smooth theme toggle with persistent state saved in `localStorage`.
- High-contrast, vibrant visual palette:
  - **Dark Mode**: Deep space navy canvas (`#090c15`) accented with electric purple, vibrant magenta, and neon amber.
  - **Light Mode**: Crisp lavender porcelain canvas (`#f8f6fc`) with rich violet typography and deep jewel-tone accents.

### 4. About Me
- **Split-Screen Layout**: Creative card composition with Vimal Jyothi Engineering College credentials, 2027 graduation year, and animated chips: `DESIGN`, `ILLUSTRATION`, `UI/UX`, `WEB`, `CREATIVITY`.
- **Narrative**: Details Samrudha's conscious decision to pursue a career where visual artistry and human empathy lead, rather than a pure backend programming track.

### 5. Skills (No Misleading Percentage Bars)
- Visual cards with interactive hover effects and tool badges.
- **Figma Mastery (Hero Feature)**: Highlighted with Figma auto-layout visual widgets, design token systems, interactive prototyping, and component architecture.
